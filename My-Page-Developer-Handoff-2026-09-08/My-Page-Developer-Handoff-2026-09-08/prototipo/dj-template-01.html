About NOA
Rhythm is a meeting point between memory, movement and the people in front of me.

NOA is a Cape Verdean DJ and producer shaping sets around Afro House, Amapiano and electronic music. Her performances move between intimate rooms, beach stages and festival crowds while keeping percussion and atmosphere at the center.
